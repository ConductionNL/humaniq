# Proposal: hours-week-line-hours-in-bold

## Why

Board DqZaak and DqZaakMeer "Uren registreren" (r39): the week line reads "Week 41 · 5 tot en met 11 okt · **11,5 uur** op deze zaak" at 14px, with the hours on the case in bold. Live drew the line at 13px without the bold hours, and the booking form's labels at 15px and 700 on a 22.5 line, so the form's first row sat 13.5 under the title against the board's 16.

## What changes

- The week line names the object's hours in bold, inside the translated sentence: the sentence is translated with a mark for the hours and split on it, so each language keeps its own word order.
- On a host's board look the week line is 14px, the form's labels 14px at 600 on the normal line, 6px above their field, and the fields 12px apart.
